Origami's version converts the original to glTF and uses meshopt compression and WebP textures. Frame Studio replaces the display texture with the user's screenshot at runtime, projects screen UVs, normalizes scale and orientation, adjusts body and lens materials, and replaces the original transparent cover with a calibrated dielectric display material. Geometry is otherwise preserved. The old procedural handset has been removed.
