Origami's version converts the original to glTF and uses meshopt compression and WebP textures. Frame Studio replaces the display texture with the user's screenshot at runtime, projects screen UVs, normalizes scale and orientation, adjusts body and lens materials, and replaces the original transparent cover with a calibrated dielectric display material. Original island and camera meshes use opaque non-reflective materials to close the bezel openings. The 17 Pro option adapts this mesh to a smaller body and is not a separate exact model. The old procedural handset has been removed.
